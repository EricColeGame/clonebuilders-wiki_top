## What the Official Description Actually Advertises

The core loop is stated plainly: give your clones an idea, and they build. The developers describe a structured building system in which clones place, resize, move, and assemble parts. That structure is the important detail. Rather than dropping a fully formed model, the clones work through a visible sequence of construction steps, which is why the game reads more like a laboratory demonstration than a conventional tycoon.

The official page also frames the project as a testing ground. Features are expected to change constantly, and the description states that experiments inside this experience may reach the developers' future games. That means you should treat any arrangement you build as potentially temporary — not because your work is deleted, but because the systems driving the clones can shift between sessions.

A final advertised point that many players overlook: private servers are recommended for large builds and minigames. The official record lists up to 15 players per server, and with about 4.2 million visits logged as of 2026-10-14, public servers can fill quickly.
